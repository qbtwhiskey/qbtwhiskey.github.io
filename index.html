Open Chair Policy

Refined enough for a leather chair. Relaxed enough for anyone to sit down.

The Bourbon Table began after work, with a few glasses and a couple of bottles someone thought the rest of us needed to try. A quick tasting became a rotation, the rotation became a handwritten binder, and that binder started moving from house to house. Sometimes it lived at the cigar club, where others could flip through the pages, laugh at the notes, and follow along with the pours we had already argued over.

The Table

A Central Illinois rotation with room for every kind of whiskey drinker.

We come from different working lives: IT, business ownership, financial investment, industry, and police work. Whether you work behind a desk, own the business, run the numbers, walk the beat, work with your hands, or just enjoy a good pour, there is a chair for you here.

Some nights are blind tastings. Some are all bourbon, all rye, a mixed lineup, or a scotch that slipped into the room. The host sets the format, the table brings opinions, and the handwritten pages tell the story after the pour is gone.

How We Review

Built from paper notes, cleaned up for digital reading.

The original reviews lived on paper in a handwritten binder that passed between houses and occasionally sat at the cigar club for others to read. For the public site, the experience is intentionally our own: searchable bottle records, standardized tasting language, clear review status, and a path to compare similar whiskeys without needing to know the jargon first.

Look Color, proof, date, price, and bottle context where available.
Nose First impressions and shared descriptors from the table.
Palate Sweetness, spice, oak, fruit, texture, proof, and balance.
Finish Length, heat, aftertaste, and whether anyone reached back for more.
